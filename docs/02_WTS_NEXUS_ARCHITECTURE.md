## What The Nexus Is

The Nexus is not a social feed.
It is an operating system for traders.

It surfaces intelligence, not content.
It shows verified performance, not screenshots.
It feels alive because it IS alive — driven by real market events and real robot activity.

## WTS IQ — The Reputation Score

Not followers. Not likes. Not views.

WTS IQ is calculated from:
- Mission success rate
- Robot live performance
- Risk management consistency
- Community contribution quality
- Backtesting accuracy
- Marketplace ratings
- Teaching impact

Range: 0 – 10,000
This is the number traders respect.

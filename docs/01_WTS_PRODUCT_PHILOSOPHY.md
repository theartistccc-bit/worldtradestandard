# WTS Product Philosophy
## The DNA of WorldTradeStandard

---

## Core Principle: Intent-First Design

WTS is built around **human intent**, not features.

**Wrong approach (feature-first):**
- Charts tab
- Orders tab  
- Positions tab
- Indicators tab

**Right approach (intent-first):**
- "Watch Gold"
- "Build a robot"
- "Analyze EURUSD"
- "Backtest this strategy"

Every UI decision, every feature, every screen must answer:
**What is the trader trying to accomplish?**

Not: what button should we build?

---

## The Three Laws of WTS Design

**Law 1 — Intelligence over content**
The app should surface intelligence, not posts.
Every screen should answer: what does the trader need to know right now?

**Law 2 — The app should never feel empty**
Something is always happening. Every few seconds:
- A mission completes
- A market moves
- A robot updates
- A challenge launches
- An AI insight fires

**Law 3 — The relationship is the product**
WTS stops being software when the AI Trading Companion
knows the trader's goals, history, mistakes, and markets.
At that point WTS feels like a trading partner, not a tool.

---

## What WTS Is Not

- Not a social media platform with trading features
- Not a charting tool with a community tab
- Not a robot marketplace with a feed
- Not a copy of TradingView, MetaTrader, or Bloomberg

**WTS is an operating system for traders.**
Every feature exists to help traders think, decide, and execute better.

---

## The Companion Principle

The AI Trading Companion is not a chatbot.
It is a living assistant that persists across the entire app.

It remembers:
- The user's goals
- Their favorite markets
- Their risk tolerance
- Their active robots
- Their trade history
- Their recent mistakes
- Markets they asked it to monitor

It speaks proactively:
> "Markson, Gold has entered the order block you asked me to monitor yesterday."
> "Your EURUSD robot has underperformed for three consecutive weeks. Would you like me to optimize it?"
> "I found three creators whose robots outperform yours on NASDAQ. Would you like to compare them?"

---

## Guiding Questions for Every Feature

Before building anything, ask:
1. Does this help the trader accomplish an intent?
2. Does this make the app feel more alive?
3. Does this strengthen the trader-companion relationship?
4. Would a professional trader find this useful at 6am before the London open?

If the answer to all four is not yes — redesign it.

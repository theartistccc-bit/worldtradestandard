# WTS Gamification & Engagement Architecture
## Challenges, Reputation, and Rewards

---

## Core Principle

Engagement in WTS is earned through performance, not popularity.
Followers are meaningless. Results are everything.

---

## WTS IQ — The Universal Reputation Score

Range: 0 – 10,000

**Calculation inputs:**
| Factor | Weight |
|--------|--------|
| Mission success rate | 25% |
| Robot live performance | 20% |
| Risk management consistency | 20% |
| Backtesting accuracy (vs live) | 15% |
| Community contribution quality | 10% |
| Marketplace ratings | 5% |
| Teaching impact (others who improved) | 5% |

**Rank thresholds:**
| WTS IQ | Rank |
|--------|------|
| 0-999 | Apprentice |
| 1000-2499 | Journeyman |
| 2500-4999 | Veteran |
| 5000-7499 | Elite |
| 7500-9999 | Legend |
| 10000 | Sovereign |

WTS IQ is the number people respect on this platform.

## AI Challenges

**Daily challenges:**
- Build an EA using only one indicator
- Best pip capture on Gold in 24 hours
- Lowest drawdown while staying profitable

**Weekly challenges:**
- Best XAUUSD mission performance
- Fastest profitable scalper
- Highest Sharpe ratio on H1

**Monthly challenges:**
- Best overall portfolio risk-adjusted return
- Most consistent win rate (minimum 20 trades)
- Best new robot release (community voted)

**Rewards per challenge:**
- XP points
- WTS Credits
- Badges (permanent profile display)
- Marketplace featured placement
- WTS IQ boost
- Community recognition post

---

## Badges

Badges are earned, never purchased.

**Performance badges:**
- Gold Hunter — 10 profitable Gold missions
- Iron Discipline — 30 consecutive days within risk limits
- Drawdown Shield — Never exceeded 5% DD in 90 days
- Win Streak — 10 consecutive profitable trades
- Century Club — 100 live trades completed

**Community badges:**
- Mentor — 10 traders improved after following your robots
- Creator — First robot published to Marketplace
- Innovator — Strategy improvement > 20% documented
- Verified — WTS-verified performance (admin granted)

**Achievement badges:**
- Prop Firm Ready — Passed all prop firm simulation rules
- Oracle Approved — Backtest matched live performance within 5%
- Battle Champion — Won a Gold Battle week

---

## Battle Room Rules

**Weekly Gold Battle:**
1. All users submit price predictions for Friday close
2. Predictions locked 24 hours before close
3. Nearest prediction wins
4. Leaderboard updates live as price moves
5. Multiple prediction types: exact price / direction / range

**Prize structure:**
- 1st place: 500 Credits + Battle Champion badge + Nexus feature
- 2nd place: 200 Credits + Nexus shoutout
- 3rd place: 100 Credits
- Participation: 10 Credits + XP

## XP System

XP drives rank progression and unlocks features.

**XP sources:**
| Action | XP earned |
|--------|-----------|
| Daily login | 10 XP |
| Completed mission | 25 XP |
| Robot published | 100 XP |
| Challenge participation | 50 XP |
| Challenge win | 500 XP |
| Learning module completed | 30 XP |
| Community post liked 10+ times | 20 XP |
| Referral joined | 100 XP |

**XP unlocks:**
- 500 XP: Access to AI Debates voting
- 1000 XP: Access to Battle Room
- 2500 XP: Access to Missions Marketplace (selling)
- 5000 XP: Access to AI Challenge creation
- 10000 XP: Verified creator status application

---

## Streak System

**Daily trading streak:**
- Consecutive days with at least one mission active
- Streak bonuses: 2x XP at 7 days, 3x at 30 days, 5x at 90 days

**Win streak:**
- Consecutive profitable closed trades
- Displayed on public profile
- Contributes to WTS IQ calculation

**Discipline streak:**
- Consecutive days within risk parameters
- Never exceeded daily loss limit
- The most valuable streak — shows real edge

---

## Anonymous Mode Mechanics

When a user enables Anonymous Hedge Fund Mode:

- Username replaced with generated callsign: Operator-[3 digit number]
- Profile photo replaced with encrypted mask icon
- Real name hidden everywhere
- Only statistics visible: WTS IQ, win rate, profit factor
- Callsign persists — cannot be changed without resetting reputation
- Can revert to named profile at any time (keeps WTS IQ)

This creates a cyberpunk aesthetic that appeals to serious traders
who let performance speak instead of personality.
